  The card design is based on the research about PwD's sensitivity to touching and pictures' arousal of their old memories. The design combines multi-sensory elements: (1) a picture (vision) related to PwD's memories and (2) the fabric (touch) matching the contents of the picture. In this way, PwD can have multiple methods of finding the match. The memory training through the game can also be strengthened."
