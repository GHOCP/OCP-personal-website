<SemiTitle
  toc="Interview Results" 
  title="6. Results and Analysis: From Follow-up Interview" 
/>

<TextTwoColsNRows 
  content="
    <b>Feedback 1: Messy user interface</b>
    User interface components such as icons, the application placement, as well as the distinct compartmentalized model became a problem for some of the participants. E.g., &quot;I sometimes got confused by the icon, some of them have the text and some don’t.&quot;
    -- Code: (Q-1, P-3), (Q-2, P-1), (Q-2, P-3), (Q-2, P-4), (Q-2, P-5), (Q-4, P-1).
    
    <b>Feedback 2: No tutorial for the first time use</b>
    Two responses from the follow-up interview indicated that tutorials and guidelines are missing for the first-time user. E.g., &quot;There was no tutorial or guidelines for using it.&quot;
    -- Code:(Q-1, P-1), (Q-3, P-2).

    <b>Feedback 3: Have proper customization</b>
    Several participants said customization and personalization were not essential to be implemented into the application. One participant mentioned the un-necessity of the dynamic App localization. E.g., &quot;Customization is not that important or crucial for student, apps & tiles position should be in a fixed position.&quot;
    -- Code:(Q-4, P-2), (Q-4, P-3),(Q-4, P-4).

    <b>Feedback 4: Some functions are not working</b>
    Some of the application's functions cannot work correctly. This could affect the effectiveness (although those functions that are not working were not tested in this research). E.g., &quot;Some of the features are not working at all, 404 page not found.&quot;
    -- Code:(Q-1, P-2), (Q-1, P-4).
  "
