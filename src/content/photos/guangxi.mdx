  content="This was a five-day trip in Guangxi. First time bringing my sweetheart Brompton to another province by train. This is a testament to me that owning a bike expands my view of the world. Take the bike with you on public transportation, arrive, and ride. It is different from traveling by car. Every pedal, steering, braking, coasting, climbing, speeding, sweating, peeking, listening, … your dynamic interaction with your surroundings, enabled by a mechanical creation, a bike, tells you the flow of nature and the flow of life. I think this is the biggest meaning of cycling, or “ride-to-travel,” to me.
  
  My route was Guilin -> Daxu -> Xingping -> Yangshuo -> Guilin, with around 200 km. The total distance was way smaller than I expected. Given my heavy luggage and the fact that it was a leisure ride, the mileage wasn't bad. The best part was cycling between cities. The scenic countryside views on the roadside and the swing-by of the unknown villages brought me huge excitement. The cities were often crowded with tourists and vehicles. The overdevelopment in tourism caused a little disappointment. I guess it was quite common in most of the Chinese tourist cities."
