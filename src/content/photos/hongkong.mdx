  content="My first time visiting Hong Kong with a friend. A short trip driven by our desire to watch the premiere of Avengers III. Honestly, it was just so-so. I almost felt a bit of disappointment. It was quite the stereotypical landscape, as in those I've known for a long time from the Hong Kong movies - Hong Kong is a giant supermarket, one that is swarming with Chow Tai Fook jewelry. Strangers can buy everything and leave. Never expect to find a sense of belonging here. It is hard to shape your memories and emotional ties. But I can still conceive of something special after meeting and talking with some Hongkongers, like the kind bookstore owner. It is related to a great spirit, one that guides people to live prudently and determinedly, with unspoken pain. While this natural acquisition of reality renders part of the Hong Kong spirit, it may also strip a bit of the astral world they are eager to learn about."
