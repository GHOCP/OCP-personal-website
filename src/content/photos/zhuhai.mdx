  content="A seaside city is probably the most appropriate place to visit together with someone you cherish. Zhuhai is not a city of beauty, but it has the least arrogance among those &quot;developed cities&quot; in Guangdong. It must be the sea that dampens the nerves and anxiety.
  
  From the perspective of treating Zhuhai as a city still advancing, part of the courtesy, respect, and smiles we met here can be inferred as a requirement and a polite formula of professionalism. But to some degree, &quot;service awareness&quot; intrinsically harbours occupational compulsoriness, not &quot;from the bottom of one's heart&quot;.
  
  Anyway, I enjoyed myself on this little trip, comfortably. The not-too-blue sea, not-too-blue sky, not-too-crowded streets, and the everywhere &quot;smiling service&quot;... Most importantly, there is the sea, and there are wonderful people."
